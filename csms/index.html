CSM onboarding

Get learners into TMS, keep enrollments right, and report back to clients.

Audience
Client success managers
Time
About 30 minutes

Your first 30 minutes

  1. Sign in to TMS and check you can see the Admin menu. 4 min
  2. Read How learners get enrolled: roster CSV or training key. 5 min
  3. Read Enroll and manage learners, especially the billing warning. 8 min
  4. Skim the launch checklist so you know what happens when. 8 min
  5. Save the welcome email for your first program. 3 min

Five things to know

1

Enrolled is what Skillable bills for

And the status can’t be changed once the class starts. Remove anyone who isn’t coming before the start date.

2

One email, one account

Never create a second account for someone. Find the existing one, or ask the lab admin to change the email.

3

Two ways to enroll

A roster CSV for most programs. A training key for very large groups, with more follow-up.

4

Learners come to you first

You handle access and sign-in problems. Lab problems go to the lab admin.

5

Every extra hour of lab time costs money

Check with the lab admin before offering a client extra access after the course ends.
