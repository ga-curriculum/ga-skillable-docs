Learner welcome email

A ready-to-send welcome that gets every learner signed in and through the tech check before day one.

Audience
CSMs
Time
3 min read

Send the email below to each learner after the roster CSV is uploaded, about 10 days before day one. Attach the learner handout. The program launch checklist shows where this fits.

Fields in {curly braces} are filled in per learner or per program before sending.

Before you send

  • Send credentials only to the learner’s own email address.
  • Keep a list of who has confirmed sign-in and the connection test. Follow up with anyone outstanding 5 days before day one.
  • If a learner replies that they can’t see their class, check their enrollment status in TMS before anything else.

The email

Subject Your lab access for {Program name}: action needed before {Start date}

Hi {First name},

Welcome to {Program name}. Your hands-on work in this program happens in Skillable, a lab platform that gives you a ready-to-use computer in your browser. You don’t need to install anything.

Your login details

  • Sign-in page:
  • Username: {Learner email}
  • Temporary password: {Temporary password}

You’ll be asked to set a new password the first time you sign in.

Please complete these two steps by {Tech check deadline}:

  1. Sign in and confirm you can see {Class name} under the Classes tab on your dashboard.
  2. Run the connection test at from the laptop and network you’ll use for the program. Please also allow pop-ups for the TMS site.

If either step doesn’t work, reply to this email and we’ll sort it out before day one. If you’re on a corporate network or VPN, your IT team may need to allow access to Skillable.

The attached quick-start guide explains how labs work, including the most important habit: saving your work to GitHub at the end of every session.

See you on {Start date}.
