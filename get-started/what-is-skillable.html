Why we use Skillable

Real tools, identical environments from the first minute, and instructors who can see what’s happening.

Audience
Anyone at GA new to Skillable, and CSMs explaining the platform to clients
Time
5 min read

Skillable is a hands-on lab platform. It hosts virtual machines and cloud environments that learners use from a browser, and software vendors and training providers use it to deliver real-environment practice. GA uses it to run custom labs we design and build ourselves for our client programs.

The four things everyone should know

1

Skillable is two products

Skillable Studio is where labs are built. Skillable TMS is where classes, rosters, instructors and learners live. Learners only ever see TMS and the lab window.

2

Everyone uses TMS. Studio is by arrangement

GA has four Studio licenses. Lauren holds one as admin and builder. The other three rotate to SMEs, LEDs or instructors who are actively building a course.

3

Lab machines are disposable by default

A lab is a fresh copy of a saved image every time it launches. Anything a learner doesn’t push to GitHub (or save, where saving is on) can be lost.

4

GA builds its own environments

We design and maintain the machine images, lab instructions and settings ourselves. Requests for changes come to GA first, not Skillable.

What Skillable gives us

Our programs teach people to build real things with real tools. That only works if every learner has a working, identical environment from the first minute, and if instructors can see what’s happening when something goes wrong. Skillable gives us both, in a browser, with environments GA designs ourselves.

Nothing to install on learner laptops

Learners open a lab in their browser and get a full machine with every tool already set up. Locked-down corporate laptops, admin rights and VPNs stop being a problem on day one.

The same environment for everyone

Every learner starts from the same GA-built image, with the same tool versions and settings. Instructors teach one setup instead of debugging twenty.

Labs built for our curriculum

GA designs the machines, writes the lab instructions and sets the rules. We aren’t limited to a vendor’s catalog, and we can change a lab when the curriculum changes.

Instructors can see and help

Instructors see every running lab in their class, watch a learner’s screen live, and take control to fix a problem together.

Checks and reporting built in

Labs can include questions and automated checks, with feedback. CSMs can report on who launched, how long they worked and what they completed.

Control over access and time

GA enrolls learners, sets when labs open and close, and can grant extra lab time after a course ends, down to the hour.

A platform built for this

Skillable is an established hands-on lab platform with 24/7 support. GA has priority support with our own support address.

The key shift

With Skillable, if something’s wrong in the lab, it’s usually ours to fix. That’s more control and more responsibility.

What it asks of us

Skillable gives us more control, and with that comes more responsibility:

  • Lab machines are disposable by default. Learners need to push their work to GitHub. We teach this from day one.
  • GA owns the lab environment. If a tool is missing or a lab misbehaves, it’s usually ours to fix. The lab admin handles that.
  • Setup takes lead time. Rosters, lab builds and testing happen before day one. See the program launch checklist.
