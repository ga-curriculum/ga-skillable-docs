The roles

Role In one line Works in
Lab admin Manages the Skillable relationship, builds the lab environments and holds the admin Studio license Skillable Studio, TMS
Studio builder An SME, LED or instructor building a course with the lab admin, on one of three shared Studio licenses Skillable Studio, by arrangement
CSM Owns the client relationship, enrollment and reporting TMS (Operations Manager role)
Instructor / TA Delivers sessions and helps learners in their labs TMS (Instructor role)
LED Designs the learning experience and writes lab instructions Markdown, outside Skillable
SME Owns technical accuracy and the lab environment: tools, packages, cloud services, access and credentials Tech requirements, outside Skillable
Skillable support Fixes platform issues, through GA’s priority support address; 24/7 Email, then Skillable’s support site
Learner Does the work and pushes it to GitHub TMS, lab window

Rules of thumb

Anything inside Skillable Studio

Goes to the lab admin, or gets built together in a working session.

Anything about who’s enrolled

Goes to the CSM.

Anything wrong in the lab instructions

Goes to the LED or SME, who sends the fix to the lab admin.

Platform problems or after hours

Email GA’s Skillable priority support address first. Open a ticket only if you can’t reach anyone. Then tell the lab admin.
