Open your class

The quickest way to a class you’re teaching is your calendar.

  1. Select My Calendar at the top of any page in TMS.
  2. Select the class name. This opens the Class page.

You can also select My Classes in the top navigation to see your current classes.

What’s on the Class page

The Class page is your home base for a class. It shows:

  • The class dates and session times
  • The roster: who is registered and their enrollment status
  • The course and its labs, which you can launch from here
  • The virtual classroom link, if the class uses one
  • Monitor Labs, your view of every running lab during class (see During class)

Check the roster

Look at the roster a day or two before class so you know who should be there.

  • Only learners with the status Enrolled (or Audit) can launch labs when class starts.
  • Someone missing? Don’t create an account for them. Send their name and email to the program’s CSM.
  • Someone you don’t expect? Tell the CSM. Skillable bills GA for every learner marked Enrolled.

Your instructor dashboard

If your account has the Instructor Dashboard, it summarizes today’s sessions (with a chart of how far learners have got), this week’s classes and your month at a glance.

Test the lab before you teach it

Launch every lab yourself before learners do. You can launch it from the Class page.

  • Walk the setup steps exactly as learners will.
  • Run the tools the lab uses.
  • Note the lab duration and timeouts so you can tell learners at the start of the session.

Instructors can keep up to 5 saved labs (each kept for 7 days) for demos and prep. Your test lab is disposable too, so changes you make never reach learners. If something in the lab is wrong, tell the lab admin.

For a full checklist, see Test a lab.
