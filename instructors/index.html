Instructor onboarding

Get ready to teach on Skillable in about 30 minutes, then keep two pages open while you teach.

Audience
Instructors and TAs
Time
About 30 minutes

Your first 30 minutes

  1. Sign in to TMS and set your password. 4 min
  2. Find your class on My Calendar and check the roster. 5 min
  3. Launch your lab and walk through it as a learner would. Note how long it runs. 10 min
  4. Read How labs work. It explains almost every question learners will ask. 10 min
  5. Bookmark During class and When something goes wrong.

Five things to know

1

The machine is a copy, GitHub is the backup

Each launch is a fresh copy of the lab image. Work that isn’t pushed to GitHub can be lost when the lab ends.

2

You can see every running lab

Monitor Labs shows each learner’s lab and how long since they were last active. You can watch or take control.

3

Keys and settings don’t carry over

Anything a learner sets by hand, such as an environment variable, is gone on a fresh machine. See the FAQ.

4

Credentials never go in chat or on slides

Keys learners need are built into the lab or set at launch. If one is missing, tell the lab admin.

5

Problems come to GA first

The CSM for access, the lab admin for anything in the lab, and GA’s Skillable priority support address for platform problems.
