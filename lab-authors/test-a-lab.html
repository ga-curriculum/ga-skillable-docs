Test a lab before learners do

What to check, how to walk a lab end to end, and how to report what you find.

Audience
Contractors, LEDs, SMEs and instructors
Time
6 min read

Testing a lab means launching it the way a learner would and working through every page. Skillable’s special syntax only renders inside a real lab, not in GitHub or VS Code previews, so a real launch is the only way to see what learners will see.

Never signed in to TMS? Start with Sign in to TMS. Contractors: The lab admin will tell you which class and lab to test.

Before you start

  • Know which program, class and lab you’re testing. The lab admin or the LED who asked you will tell you.
  • Have a place to take notes and screenshots. The lab machine is deleted when the lab ends.
  • Remember that your test lab is disposable. Changes you make in it never reach learners.

Walk the lab

Launch the lab and wait for the machine to start (a minute or two). Then work through it page by page and tick things off as you go.

Report what you find

Send your notes to the right person.

What you found Send it to
Wrong or unclear instructions The LED or SME who owns the course. They send the fix to the lab admin.
A missing tool, a broken machine, wrong lab settings The Lab admin

Include:

  • Program and lab name, and the page or step
  • What you expected and what happened
  • A screenshot
  • The lab instance ID, from the lab’s Help panel. Take a screenshot while the lab is still open.

For instruction changes, send the updated Markdown rather than a description of the edit. The full process is on Request a lab change.

Don’t edit instructions in a test lab and expect the change to stick. Test sessions are disposable too.
